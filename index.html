The Chef

A small counter, a big respect for tradition

Q Sushi opened in 2026 in the Milk District — soft-opening in May, grand opening on September 12 — in the former Pom Pom's space on Bumby Ave. Behind the counter is Chef Hong Wong, Hong Kong-trained and formerly of Wa Sushi, serving Edomae sushi the traditional way: seasonal fish flown in from Japan, rice seasoned with dark red vinegar, house-made soy sauce.

  • ✓

    Edomae, the traditional way — red-vinegar rice, house soy, each piece seasoned by the chef.

  • ✓

    Flown in from Japan — Hokkaido uni, hakkaku, cuttlefish and the best of the season.

  • ✓

    Eight seats, one seating at a time — a husband-and-wife team, and nothing between you and the chef.
